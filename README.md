# Awesome-SaaS-Demo-Trial-Management

## Top SaaS Demo & Trial Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Interactive Product Demos, Trial Environments & Self-Hosted Demo Automation*

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **commercial SaaS demo and trial management platforms** and **open-source projects** that help software companies create interactive product demos, manage trial environments, and convert prospects through hands-on product experiences.



**Examples** include Salesforce AppExchange Test Drive, Navattic, Reprise, Demostack, Walnut, Tourial, Storylane, Saleo, Arcade, and TestBox (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Demo and trial management is a rapidly growing open-source domain. **Propels** leads as an open-source interactive demo builder that replaces static "Book a Demo" buttons with hands-on product experiences . **LiveDemo** delivers a self-hosted alternative to Storylane, Navattic, and Arcade with AI voiceovers, lead capture, and engagement analytics . **AutoDemo** treats demos as code — YAML scenarios in your repo, regenerated by CI so they never go stale . **DemoKit** intercepts API calls to return mock data, turning your real app into an interactive demo without backend changes . **DemoDSL** generates narrated demo videos from YAML/JSON definitions . **sales.demos** brings demo automation as code with Claude Code skills and AAP job templates .



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



### Interactive Demo Builders



- **[Propels](https://github.com/Propels-AI/Propels)**  

  **Open-source interactive demo builder**, AGPL-3.0 licensed . **Turn your website visitors into revenue with interactive product demos** . **Replaces static "Book a Demo" buttons with engaging, interactive product demos** . **Chrome extension records interactive demos** of your product . **Embed demos directly on marketing blog or landing page** . **Built-in lead form captures high-intent leads** who have already seen the value . **Tech stack**: React, AWS Amplify backend . **Best for open-source interactive demo creation** .



- **[LiveDemo](https://github.com/exploitx3/livedemo-deploy)**  

  **Open-source alternative to Storylane, Navattic, and Arcade**, open-source . **Create interactive product demos in minutes** — capture any workflow, add AI voiceovers and personalized text . **Share as link, embed, GIF, or video** while tracking engagement and collecting leads across website, docs, and sales outreach . **Self-hostable with full data control** . **AI voice engine** converts text to natural speech in multiple languages . **Analytics** — view counts, click heatmaps, and lead capture . **Best for self-hosted interactive demos with AI voiceovers** .



- **[AutoDemo](https://github.com/praveen-palanisamy/autodemo)**  

  **Demos as code — generate demo videos, interactive walkthroughs, and marketing captures from your running web app**, MIT licensed . **One command drives a real browser through your real app**: `autodemo demo "Sign up and open the dashboard" --url http://localhost:3000` . **Produces**: video.mp4 (narrated demo video), index.html (static, embeddable interactive walkthrough), assets/*.png (named marketing captures), run.json (reproducible metadata) . **Versioned** — scenarios are YAML in your repo, reviewed like code . **Reproducible** — deterministic Playwright steps, or AI steps when you want speed . **Continuous** — GitHub Action regenerates everything on merge . **Yours** — runs on your machine and CI; no cloud, no telemetry . **Best for CI-native, agent-ready demo automation** .



- **[DemoDSL](https://github.com/Fran-cois/demodsl)**  

  **DSL-driven automated product demo video generator**, open-source . **Define product demos in YAML/JSON, get a narrated, subtitled walkthrough video** . **Semantic beats** — describe a step by intent and let the engine pick camera framing, pointing gesture, and pacing . **Effect library with 27+ reusable presets** (callouts, intros, CTAs, dataviz, transitions) . **Selector-driven layout with anchors** — declare anchors and let DemoDSL probe the live page via Playwright . **Reliability & style** — `on_error` handling, reproducible seeds, validated themes . **Best for DSL-driven demo video generation** .



### Demo Environment & Trial Management



- **[DemoKit](https://github.com/Kasava-AI/demokit)**  

  **Open-source SDK that turns your real app into an interactive demo by intercepting API calls and returning mock data**, open-source . **Zero backend changes** — intercepts fetch calls at the client level . **Framework adapters**: React, Next.js, Remix, React Router, TanStack Query, SWR, tRPC . **LLM fixture generation** — generate realistic demo data with your own API keys (BYOK) . **Session persistence** — demo mode survives page refreshes . **Self-hostable dashboard** — full web UI for managing fixtures . **Interactive demos convert 2-3x better than static content** . **Best for turning real apps into interactive demos without backend changes** .



- **[sales.demos](https://github.com/ericcames/sales.demos)**  

  **Sales demo automation as code**, open-source . **Every demo runs two ways from the same playbooks** — as a Claude Code skill on your laptop, or as a job template inside Ansible Automation Platform . **What you rehearse is literally what you present** . **Disposable environments, persistent configuration** — when a cluster expires, repoint three variables and the demo comes back exactly as it was . **Five repeatable demos across three environments** (sandbox, demo, edge) . **Skills** for setup, verification, provisioning, image linking, and teardown . **Best for pre-sales engineers running repeatable, reproducible demos** .



### Demo Video & Screenshot Generation



- **[ichec-motion](https://www.npmjs.com/package/ichec-motion)**  

  **Engine for animated explainers and demo videos**, open-source . **Tech stack walkthroughs, deploy stories, and product demos with characters** . **Scenes are TypeScript, animated with Canvas Commons** (maintained fork of Motion Canvas) . **Same scenes render as standalone video or as clips in a PowerPoint deck** that a presenter clicks through . **Scene components**: panels, pipes, characters, captions, screen-recording player . **Best for animated demo videos and presenter decks** .



- **[aidemo](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@tandryukha/aidemo)**  

  **Open-source demo video generator with narrated, captioned output**, open-source . **Separates authoring (slow, one-time) from recording (fast deterministic replay)** . **Pipeline**: storyboard.json → voice (OpenAI/ElevenLabs/local TTS) → record (drives Chrome with animated cursor) → captions (Whisper) → compose (trim idle, sync, auto-zoom, cards) → output/final-demo.mp4 . **CI-native** — GitHub Action keeps demos in sync on every relevant change . **No API key needed for local TTS** . **Best for self-maintaining demo videos in CI** .



- **[vif](https://github.com/arach/vif)**  

  **Agentic app video and screenshots generation**, open-source . **Define demo sequences declaratively in YAML** . **macOS-native** (uses built-in `screencapture`) . **Coordinate mode** automates any macOS app without SDK integration . **VifTargets SDK** for semantic target names in apps you control . **CLI**: play, record, gif, optimize, take, shot, windows . **Best for macOS app demo automation** .



- **[svg-interactive-animation-engine](https://www.npmjs.com/package/svg-interactive-animation-engine)**  

  **Lightweight, purely SVG-based interactive animation engine**, open-source . **Born to build animated assets and interactive product walkthroughs** . **60FPS requestAnimationFrame loop** manipulating SVG attributes and CSS properties . **Event-driven architecture** chains timelines, pauses for interactions, triggers parallel layer animations . **Supported animations**: move, fade, scale, rotate, typewriter text . **Best for AI-agent-generated interactive SVG walkthroughs** .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **OpenTutorial** — Spec-driven in-app tour and guidance engine with zero runtime dependencies, adapters for React/Vue/Svelte/Angular/Solid, cross-page tours, branching, analytics, and theming . **Best for in-app product tours and onboarding** .

- **demodsl** — Already listed. **YAML-driven demo video generator** .

- **@tandryukha/aidemo** — Already listed. **CI-native demo video generation** .



**Frameworks for building custom demo and trial management solutions**: Combine **Propels** or **LiveDemo** for interactive demo creation with lead capture . Use **AutoDemo** for CI-native demo generation that never goes stale . Deploy **DemoKit** for turning your real app into an interactive demo without backend changes . Integrate **DemoDSL** or **aidemo** for automated narrated demo videos . Use **sales.demos** for repeatable, code-driven demo environments . Choose **OpenTutorial** for in-app guided tours and onboarding . Note that true enterprise demo platforms with AI-powered personalization, deep analytics, and vendor-supported SLAs (Navattic, Reprise, Demostack) remain primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong interactive demo builders, demo-as-code pipelines, and trial environment foundations that require integration for complete demo and trial operations.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Demo and trial management platforms handle sensitive product data and prospect information. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, access controls, and compliance with data privacy regulations.

- **Demo environments must not expose production data** — use synthetic or anonymized data for demos. DemoKit's LLM fixture generation helps create realistic but safe demo data .

- **Demo maintenance is the hidden cost** — demos break when the product changes. AutoDemo and aidemo address this by regenerating demos in CI .

- **License considerations**: Propels uses AGPL-3.0 , AutoDemo uses MIT , DemoKit is open-source , DemoDSL is open-source , and sales.demos is open-source . Verify licensing against your use case before committing.

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong interactive demo builders, demo-as-code pipelines, and trial environment foundations, but **AI-powered personalization, deep analytics, and vendor-supported SLAs** remain primarily commercial offerings.
